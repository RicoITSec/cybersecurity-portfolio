# Phishing Incident Response & Escalation

## Objective
Perform SOC L1-style triage on a phishing alert and determine whether escalation is required.

## Investigation
- Reviewed the phishing alert.
- Validated a malicious password-protected executable.
- Documented the incident using the 5 W's framework.
- Prepared evidence for escalation.

## Outcome
The incident was treated as malicious and escalated to Level 2 with the relevant evidence and context documented for follow-up.
