# Access Control Incident Investigation

## Objective
Investigate a former contractor account that retained privileged access after the recorded end date.

## Investigation
- Reviewed account status and access context.
- Considered audit-log evidence relevant to the access-retention issue.
- Identified offboarding and privileged-access governance weaknesses.

## Recommendations
- Automate offboarding actions.
- Apply RBAC and least privilege.
- Require MFA for sensitive access.
- Strengthen privileged-access governance.
- Use dual approval for sensitive changes.
